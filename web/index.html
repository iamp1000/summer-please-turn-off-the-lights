⚡ Zero-Lag Audio Setup Guide

To achieve 100% frame-perfect acoustic sync over Wi-Fi:

  • Lookahead Compensation: Tapo Wi-Fi round-trip transit is typically 30–60ms. Setting lookahead to ~40–50ms enables our predictive beat tracker to transmit packets ahead of the downbeat.
  • Voicemeeter / VAC: Set loopback buffer to 256 or 512 samples for minimal OS audio queuing.
  • Media Player Offset: In VLC or MPV, add a +50ms audio delay so light pulses hit at the exact instant sound reaches your ears.
